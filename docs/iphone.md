# Spiel Listen for iPhone

Spiel Listen records a meeting on your iPhone and writes a live transcript, using the same
speech model as the Mac app, on the phone itself. Audio never leaves the iPhone.

## First launch

Spiel asks before downloading its speech model: a one-time download of about 600 MB, so
Wi-Fi is recommended. You can choose **Later** and download it from the home screen when
you're ready. Setup then shows two steps:

1. **Downloading**, with a percentage.
2. **Preparing for the Neural Engine.** iOS converts the model for your phone's chip. It
   happens once (and again after some iOS updates) and can take a few minutes. Keep Spiel
   open while it runs; iOS pauses it in the background.

## Recording a meeting

Tap **New meeting**. Pick a title (the meeting on your calendar right now, the date and
time, or your own), optionally add where you are, and choose whether to keep the audio.
The transcript appears a moment after each pause. Recording keeps going with the screen
locked.

## Your transcripts

Transcripts are saved on the phone, in the Files app under **On My iPhone → Spiel**. Open
one in the app to search it, copy it or share it.

- They are included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or your Mac).
- **Deleting the app deletes them.** Copy the Transcripts folder somewhere else first if you
  want to keep them.

## Run a Shortcut when Listen stops

⋯ menu → **Shortcut When Listen Stops**. See [the Shortcuts guide](shortcuts.md).
