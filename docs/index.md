# Spiel

Dictation for the Mac that runs entirely on the Mac. Press a shortcut, talk, and the text
goes into whatever app you're using. No account, no subscription, nothing sent anywhere.

![Spiel dictating into a text field](assets/spiel-v2-demo.gif)

- **Dictation.** ⌘⇧D to start and stop, or hold to talk. Works in any app.
- **Listen.** ⌘⇧L records a whole meeting or talk to a Markdown transcript with timestamps.
- **Screenshots.** ⌃⌥⌘S during Listen drops a screenshot into the transcript at that moment.
- **Shortcuts.** Run one of your Shortcuts automatically every time Listen stops.
- **Vocabulary.** Teach it names and jargon it gets wrong.
- **History.** Your last 50 dictations, searchable.
- **Command line.** `spiel transcribe meeting.m4a` turns any audio or video file into text.
- **iPhone.** Spiel Listen records meetings on your phone, the same way.

Speech recognition is NVIDIA's Parakeet running on the Neural Engine. The Mac app needs an
Apple silicon Mac (M1 or later) and macOS 14 or later.
