# Getting started

## Install

1. Download Spiel and drag it to Applications.
2. Open it. A microphone icon appears in the menu bar, and the **Set up Spiel** window opens.

## Set up Spiel

The setup window walks you through everything Spiel needs, one step at a time:

- **Microphone**, so Spiel can hear you.
- **Accessibility**, so Spiel can type into other apps. Turn Spiel on in System Settings →
  Privacy & Security → Accessibility; the step ticks itself within a second.
- **Notifications**, so Spiel can tell you when something goes wrong.
- **The speech model**, a one-time download of about 600 MB, then a short "loading onto
  the Neural Engine" step. After that Spiel works with no internet.
- **Screen Recording** (optional), only needed for [screenshots in Listen](listen.md#screenshots).

You can reopen it any time from the menu: Help → **Setup…**.

## Dictate

1. Click into any text field, in any app.
2. Press **⌘⇧D** and talk.
3. Press **⌘⇧D** again. The text lands where your cursor is.

Prefer to hold the key while you talk? Settings → General → Dictation → hold-to-talk.

## Settings

Menu bar icon → **Settings…**

- **Shortcuts:** change the Dictation, Listen and Screenshot keys.
- **Microphone:** the system default, or a specific mic. If that mic is unplugged, Spiel
  falls back to the default and tells you.
- **Speech engine:** Unified (the default), v3, or Apple's built-in engine.
- **When Listen stops:** [run one of your Shortcuts](shortcuts.md).
- **History**, **Open at Login**, **Diagnostic Logging**.
- **Vocabulary** tab: names and jargon Spiel should spell your way.

## Your license

Spiel runs free for 14 days. To keep using it after that, buy a license on
[Gumroad](https://morehavoc.gumroad.com/l/spiel), then menu → **Enter License Key** and paste
the key from your receipt email. One license covers 2 Macs.
