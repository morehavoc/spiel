# Privacy policy

_Last updated: October 27, 2026_

Spiel runs entirely on your device.

- **Your audio and your transcripts never leave your Mac or iPhone.** There is no server
  that receives them.
- There is no account, no analytics and no tracking.
- **The Mac app** connects to the internet for three things only: downloading the speech
  model on first launch (from Hugging Face), checking your license key with Gumroad (at most
  once a week), and checking GitHub for updates. None of these sends audio or anything you said.
- **The iPhone app** connects to the internet only to download the speech model (from Hugging
  Face). It uses your calendar only to suggest a meeting title, and your location only when you
  turn on **Add location** for a meeting; both stay on the phone and are saved only into that
  meeting's transcript.
- **Shortcuts:** if you choose a Shortcut to run when Listen stops, Spiel hands the transcript
  to it on your device. What that Shortcut does with it is up to you.
- **Purchases** are handled by Gumroad, the merchant of record. Gumroad's
  [privacy policy](https://gumroad.com/privacy) covers your payment and email details.
- **Diagnostics:** Help → Send Diagnostics on the Mac creates a zip with logs, app and system
  versions and permission states. It does not include anything you dictated, your
  transcripts or your vocabulary. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

Questions: see [Support](support.md).
